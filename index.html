Age groups follow the UCI gravel and gran fondo bands: 19–34, then five-year groups. UCI counts age on December 31; Athlinks gives race-day age, so a rider with a birthday between race day and year-end may sit one group younger here. Under 19 is not a UCI qualifying group. Non-binary finishers (one each in the 50 and 100) are counted under All. The elite men and women raced as separate courses and are combined in the elite tab. DNF riders are excluded from every time statistic. Home state or country is what riders entered at registration; Puerto Rico and DC count as US but not as states.

Results from Athlinks (2026 Life Time Big Sugar Gravel, event 1144157), 1,916 starters. Rider names are left out. The underlying data is in data/big_sugar_results.csv.
